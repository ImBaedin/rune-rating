Proprietary account progression score

Make progress feel like a metal you can hold.

Rune Rating should be a decomposable 0-1000 account progression score: broad enough to respect skilling, bossing, quests, diaries, collection log, clues, efficiency, and recent activity, but simple enough for a player to understand at a glance.

Public promise

Fair, not flat

A maxed skiller, PvM specialist, and completionist can all climb, but one-dimensional accounts hit visible soft caps.

Data policy

Current snapshot

Successful refreshes replace prior RuneRating snapshots. WOM history is fetched or cached only as provider-owned context.

Player identity

Current RSN

The rating follows the normalized current display name, not inferred account continuity through name changes.

Formula

Seven pillars, one visible grade.

Internally, Rune Rating is a weighted sum of capped pillar scores. Externally, players see the metal tier, score, confidence, strongest pillars, weakest pillars, and the next few realistic upgrades.

RR = round(coverageConfidence * sum(pillarScorei))

Each pillar uses saturating curves, category caps, and mode-specific calibration so a single farmed metric cannot dominate the score.

01 / 260 pts

Skill mastery

Hiscores-owned

Uses official skill levels, XP, and ranks. Score level breadth first, then XP depth with diminishing returns after 13.03m and 50m per skill. Overall XP matters, but the balance bonus rewards accounts with many developed skills instead of one extreme 200m stack.

02 / 190 pts

Bossing and combat proof

Hiscores + RuneProfile

Combines boss KC breadth, boss KC depth, combat achievements, and high-signal PvM unlocks. KC transforms with log1p style curves so 10,000 easy kills are less valuable than broad, difficult coverage. Combat achievement task tiers provide completion quality where RuneProfile data is available.

03 / 150 pts

World unlocks

RuneProfile-owned

Quests, quest points, achievement diary tiers, and account-defining unlocks become the score's proof of world access. This keeps the rating from becoming a pure XP or KC race.

04 / 140 pts

Collections, clues, and minigames

Mixed ownership

Hiscores supplies clue completions and ranked minigame scores. RuneProfile supplies granular collection-log completion when the snapshot is complete. Absence from complete collection rows means false; absence from incomplete data means unknown.

05 / 120 pts

Efficiency signal

Wise Old Man-owned

EHP, EHB, time-to-max, and time-to-200m are powerful secondary signals, but they should not overpower actual account breadth. Treat efficiency as a quality multiplier and additive pillar, calibrated separately for mains, irons, HCIM, UIM, and group variants.

06 / 90 pts

Momentum

Provider history only

Recent XP, EHP, EHB, bossing, and clue gains make the score feel alive. Fetch the provider-owned history when needed, cache transformed overview data briefly, and never persist RuneRating player history.

07 / 50 pts

Balance and account shape

Derived canonical

A small proprietary adjustment rewards well-rounded progress and flags unusual outliers. This is where RuneRating can feel distinctive without hiding the main pillars.

Exportable share card

Give players a card they want to post in Discord.

Rune Rating should include a one-click exportable account card built for clan chats, Discord, Twitter/X, Reddit, and personal profile pages. The card is a compact flex: account rating, metal tier, our custom generated art, and a short set of prestige stats.

Export formats

PNG for quick sharing, square and story crops, plus a public card URL with Open Graph metadata so pasted links unfurl cleanly.

Visual identity

Use custom RuneRating images: metal crest, tier background, class-style account artwork, pillar sigils, and seasonal trims.

Prestige stat slots

Show four to six stats: total level, total XP, rank percentile, EHP/EHB, quest cape state, diary tier, combat achievement tier, or collection completion.

Player control

Let players pick a theme, crop, and stat loadout. Keep the score, source freshness, formula version, and provisional state locked.

Implementation note

Generate cards from canonical snapshots on the server side or in a controlled render worker so private provider credentials and fetch logic stay in Convex actions. The exported image should include a timestamp and formula version, but it should not imply stored RuneRating history.

Calibration plan

Start deterministic, then tune with observed population.

1. Seed weights

Launch with hand-tuned caps and curves based on known game structure.

2. Build aggregates

Store formula versions and aggregate quantiles, not player history.

3. Freeze seasons

Rebalance only on formula versions so score changes are explainable.

Confidence

Do not rank unknowns as bad.

If RuneProfile is unavailable, publish a provisional band. If WOM is unavailable, omit efficiency and momentum from the verified score until a source policy says otherwise. Missing is not zero.

Source map

Keep the proprietary layer above canonical models.

Hiscores

Skills, rank, XP, clues, boss KC, ranked activities, and minigames. This source stays authoritative for every metric it shares.

RuneProfile

Quests, diaries, combat achievement tasks, and collection-log details. Rich categories must remain granular and searchable.

Wise Old Man

EHP, EHB, time estimates, gains, and timelines. Provider-owned history can power momentum without becoming RuneRating history.

Rule Why it matters Player-facing copy
Separate mode ladders Mains, irons, HCIM, UIM, and groups have different effort curves. "Dragon Ironman, top 8% of tracked irons."
Saturating curves Prevents single-metric farming from swamping account breadth. "More KC helps, but new boss coverage helps more."
Formula versions OSRS changes over time; ratings need auditable recalibration. "Scored with Season 1 rules."
Confidence gates Unavailable rich data should not become a hidden penalty. "Estimated Adamant-Rune until collection data refreshes."

Recommendation

Ship the score as a product surface, not just math.

The strongest version of Rune Rating is not only a number. It is a crest, a tier, a confidence label, a seven-pillar breakdown, and three next upgrades, capped by an exportable card players can share with friends and clan members. That gives players a reason to refresh, compare, and come back without making the algorithm feel arbitrary.

Research notes

  • Official-style Hiscores Lite responses expose rank, level, XP, and activity score data, matching the repo's current Hiscores SDK shape.
  • Wise Old Man exposes player endpoints, gains, records, snapshots, timelines, EHP, and EHB, which fits efficiency and momentum.
  • The OSRS metal fantasy maps cleanly to Bronze, Iron, Steel, Black, Mithril, Adamant, Rune, and Dragon.
